# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars

# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

# THEORY
Self-driving cars are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to move and navigate with little or no human intervention. They use technologies such as Machine Learning (ML), Computer Vision, sensors, and Deep Learning to understand their surroundings and make driving decisions.

Self-driving cars use cameras, radar, LiDAR, and ultrasonic sensors to detect roads, pedestrians, other vehicles, traffic signals, and obstacles. AI algorithms analyse the collected data and help the vehicle make safe driving decisions. Examples of companies developing self-driving technology include Tesla and Waymo.

These vehicles aim to improve road safety, reduce human errors, provide convenient transportation, and support efficient traffic management. However, their performance depends on environmental conditions, sensor accuracy, and the reliability of AI systems.

# WORKING PRINCIPLE
Self-driving cars work using Artificial Intelligence, sensors, Computer Vision, and Machine Learning. The working process involves the following steps:

1. Data Collection: Cameras, radar, LiDAR, and other sensors collect information about the vehicle's surroundings, including roads, vehicles, pedestrians, and obstacles.

2. Object Detection: Computer Vision and AI algorithms identify objects such as traffic lights, road signs, pedestrians, and other vehicles.

3. Environment Mapping: The system analyses sensor data to understand the road layout, lane markings, nearby objects, and the vehicle's position.

4. Decision Making: AI algorithms process the collected information and decide whether to accelerate, brake, change lanes, or turn.

5. Path Planning: The system calculates a suitable route and plans a safe path to reach the destination while avoiding obstacles.

6. Vehicle Control: The control system sends commands to the steering, braking, and acceleration systems to move the vehicle according to the planned path.

7. Continuous Monitoring: The sensors continuously monitor the surroundings, and the AI system updates its decisions whenever road conditions or traffic situations change.

Example: When a self-driving car detects a pedestrian crossing the road, its AI system analyses the situation and can apply the brakes to avoid a collision.

Thus, self-driving cars combine AI and sensor technologies to navigate roads, detect obstacles, and make driving decisions.
